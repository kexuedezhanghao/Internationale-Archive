# Charles H. Kerr 英译：1900年12月《International Socialist Review》刊本

底本：第1卷第6号，1900年12月，期末两页《The International Party》。两张谱页均无印刷页码；在本次下载的64页PDF中为第63–64页。

来源：[期刊目录](https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/isr/index.htm) · [原刊扫描PDF](https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/isr/v01n06-dec-1900-ISR-gog-Wisc.pdf)。原刊署“French Words by Eugene Pottier. Translated by Charles H. Kerr.”。

阅读转录合并乐谱中的音节连字符，按两行谱词分开还原第1、2段；不改写措辞。撇号统一为ASCII，引号统一为直引号。副歌在谱上有重复，以下列一次；法文副歌、音符和排版不在本次转录范围。原刊第3段引号开合不齐，照存，不补入网页的引号或复数。

## 歌词

```text
Arise, ye pris'ners of starvation!
Arise, ye wretched of the earth,
For justice thunders condemnation,
A better world's in birth.
No more tradition's chains shall bind us,
Arise, ye slaves! no more in thrall!
The earth shall rise on new foundations,
We have been naught, we shall be all.

We want no condescending saviors,
To rule us from a judgment hall,
We workers ask not for their favors;
Let us consult for all.
To make the thief disgorge his booty,
To free the spirit from its cell,
We must ourselves decide our duty,
We must decide and do it well.

Refrain:
'Tis the final conflict,
Let each stand in his place,
The International Party
Shall be the human race.

The law oppresses us and tricks us,
Taxation drains the victim's blood;
The rich are free from obligations,
The laws the poor delude.
Too long we've languished in subjection,
Equality has other laws:
"No rights," says she, without their duties,
No claims on equals without cause."

Behold them seated in their glory,
The kings of mine and rail and soil!
What have you read in all their story,
But how they plundered toil?
Fruits of the people's work are buried
In the strong coffers of a few;
In voting for their restitution
The men will only ask their due.

Toilers from shops and fields united,
The party we of all who work;
The earth belongs to us the people,
No room here for the shirk.
How many on our flesh have fattened!
But if the noisome birds of prey
Shall vanish from the sky some morning,
The blessed sunlight still will stay.
```
